Covenant #2 With Avraham: Sign of Circumcision
This is the longest and most complicated speech so far in the book of Genesis. Before diving into its details,
it’s helpful to get an overview of its main content and literary organization.
Macro Design of Genesis 17
At its most basic, the chapter is designed in a simple three-step progression.
