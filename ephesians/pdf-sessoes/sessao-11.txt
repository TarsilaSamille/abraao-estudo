Session 11: This Age and the Age to Come

Key Takeaways
In the overlap of the ages, Jesus is King, and his resurrection was a preview of the divine power he will
demonstrate when he remakes all of creation.

Continuation of Ephesians 1:15-23
This session’s diagram depicts the spheres of the current age and the age to come and demonstrates how the
two merged in the death and resurrection of Jesus.
