Session 3: Paul’s Apocalypse

Key Takeaways
According to the Bible, Heaven and Earth are distinct but not separate realities.
An apocalypse is when the bond between Heaven and Earth becomes visible to us.
Paul experiences his own apocalypse when Jesus reveals himself as the risen Messiah and calls Paul to
share this revelation with others. This encounter is foundational to the book of Ephesians.
Ephesians is an invitation to experience the risen Jesus and allow that apocalypse to transform us.

The Heavens and the Earth
In the biblical worldview, the heavens and the earth are distinct but not separate realities. When Paul
encounters Jesus (Acts 9:1-6, 22:6-7, 26:13-18), God reveals this bond between Heaven and Earth to Paul in a
way that transforms his assumptions about what God is doing in the world.
