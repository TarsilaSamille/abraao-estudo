Session 20: Reflecting on the Torah

Key Takeaways
The new humanity doesn't do away with the law. Rather, it creates people who can actually live by the
just requirements of the law.
Paul views the laws of the Torah as wisdom literature, a source of divine wisdom.

This session covers the notes from sessions 19

Reflection Question
Paul has a very nuanced view of the Torah. How would you explain his view of the law? Does he see it as a
good thing, a bad thing, neither, or both?
