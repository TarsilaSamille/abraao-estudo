Session 33: Equality in the Household

Key Takeaways
Paul says the Messiah purifies the church. This imagery comes from the temple practices of ritual
purification in the Hebrew Bible.
Paul’s encounter with the risen Jesus turned his worldview upside down and made him an apocalyptic
imaginary. The heartbeat of Ephesians is how Paul scrambles the established concepts of worth, value,
and rank.
