Session 2: The Apocalypse of Jesus

Key Takeaways
The Greek word apokalypsis means to uncover or reveal—literally and metaphorically.
Ephesians summarizes the most important apocalyptic event in history: Jesus’ life, death, and
resurrection as well as the gift of the Spirit.
Chapters 1-3 of Ephesians cover how to understand the apocalypse of Jesus, and chapters 4-6 talk
about how to respond to the apocalypse of Jesus.
