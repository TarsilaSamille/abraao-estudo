Tim’s Initial Understanding of Heaven and EarthTim’s Initial Understanding of Heaven and Earth

The modern conception of the words Heaven and Earth usually have a clear meaning: The Earth is the globe and the world as a whole. The Earth is passing away, and Jesus has opened up a way for sins to be forgiven so that Christians can go to Heaven after death. Heaven is a postmortem, spiritual dimension—a disembodied paradise of eternal life with God. Sometimes, this is shared as the “biblical” view of reality. However, the Bible uses the words “heaven” and “earth” in a way that not only challenges the modern view but also points to a different biblical story.The modern conception of the words Heaven and Earth usually have a clear meaning: The Earth is the globe and the world as a whole.
