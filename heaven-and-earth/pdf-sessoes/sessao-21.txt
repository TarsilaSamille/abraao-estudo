Session 21: Jesus and Living Water

Key Takeaways
Throughout the Gospel of John, Jesus is portrayed as the true source of life—as the tabernacle dwelling
among us (John 1:14), as the temple (John 2:21), and as the garden with rivers flowing out of him (John
7:37).
The garden of Eden points to the reality of Jesus being high and lifted up, signifying that Jesus is the
source of true and eternal life.
