__TR__:Worldview__/TR__Worldview

__TR__:A worldview is a comprehensive and cosmic story through which a cultural group orders and makes sense of their life experience. Worldviews answer the basic questions of human existence. Questions such as: 1. Who are we? 2. Where are we? 3. What is the problem? 4. What is the solution? 5. Where are we located in the story? Genesis 1 is designed to answer these core questions. And we are going to tackle these questions in three large steps. 1. “Where are we?” We will examine this question by observing the world picture of Genesis 1 (i.e., cosmology). 2. “Who are we?” We will examine this question by observing the creation of humanity in Genesis 1 (i.e., anthropology). 3. “Where are we located in the story?” We will examine this question by observing the creation of time in Genesis 1 (i.e., eschatology).
