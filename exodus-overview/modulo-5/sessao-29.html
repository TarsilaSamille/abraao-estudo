Sessão 29: Reflexões sobre ÊxodoSession 29: Exodus Reflections

Key Takeaways

  • Exodus is Israel's creation story. Just like the instruction God gave the humans in the garden, God's law (torah, instruction) is a guide to living in his life-giving presence.
  • Moses' identity became clear as he was transformed in God's presence. Likewise, prayerfully meditating on the Bible shapes our lives and our identity.
  • God calls his people to be his special possession. This is a gift, an honor, and a responsibility to bear his name in a manner that reflects his character to the rest of the world.
