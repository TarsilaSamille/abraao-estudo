Key Takeaways

  • The wilderness narratives recount a liminal space for God to form Israel as a new nation.
  • The wilderness narratives in Exodus 15-18 mirror the wilderness journey of Numbers 10-21 in a chiasm that frames the entire Torah.

Literary Frame Around the Sinai Narratives

The wilderness wanderings of Exodus and Numbers form a literary frame around the Sinai narratives. On both sides of the time at Sinai, the biblical authors arranged a series of events.
