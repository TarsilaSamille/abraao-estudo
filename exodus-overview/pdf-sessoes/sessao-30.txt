Session 30: Exodus and Your Context
Key Takeaways
Slow down and wrestle through difficult parts of the Bible, considering its historical, literary, and
theological context.
Instead of a list of outdated rules, God gives his people instruction so they can participate in his own
character.
Whether in our worship or in our daily lives, we have the opportunity to clearly show people what God is
like.
