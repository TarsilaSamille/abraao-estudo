Session 3: Abraham Replays the Melody

Key Takeaways

God’s plan for all creation is worked out in the lives and relationships of generation after generation of
God’s chosen human partners.
The thematic melody of the Bible, repeated throughout Abraham’s life, prepares us to understand
Yaaqov’s story.
