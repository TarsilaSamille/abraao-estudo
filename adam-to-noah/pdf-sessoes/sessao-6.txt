Key Takeaways
The biblical authors craft the literary design of the text to make theological arguments.
Tension between God’s justice and his mercy drives the biblical storyline.

Reflection Question
Genesis 1-11 introduces a tension between God’s justice and mercy. Think of God’s just judgment and exile of
Cain in tension with the mercy of protecting Cain’s life. Is this a contradiction? What do you think this says
about the character of God?
