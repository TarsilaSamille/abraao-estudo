Session 7: Reflecting on the Macrostructure of

Key Takeaways
The Bible is meditation literature, which means some themes and connections will become apparent
only after repeated reading.
The cycle of themes from Genesis 1-11 builds anticipation for a chosen deliverer. The Hebrew Bible
points to a hope that is fulfilled in Jesus.
