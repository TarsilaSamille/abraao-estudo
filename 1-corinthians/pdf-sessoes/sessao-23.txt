Session 23: Reflecting on 1 Corinthians
Key Takeaways
Considering interpretive perspectives from different traditions than our own can help us see the text in
new ways.
1 Corinthians introduces us to Paul the person and gives us a glimpse into Paul’s heart for the people he
ministered to.
Wrestling with the text on its own terms can illuminate ways that its message applies to our lives today.
