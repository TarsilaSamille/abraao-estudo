Na história do dilúvio, a promessa de Deus de um dilúvio vindouro (hammabbulhammabbul / המבולהמבול) foi cumprida, e Deus ordenou que Noé construísse e embarcasse na arca para manter viva a sua família e os animais. Tanto o cumprimento quanto a obediência à palavra divina são registrados na narrativa.In the flood story, God’s promise of a coming flood (hammabbulhammabbul / המבולהמבול) was fulfilled, and God commanded Noah to build and board the ark to keep his family and the animals alive. The fulfillment of and the obedience to the divine word are both registered in the narrative.

Na história de Avraham, a promessa de Deus de um filho vindouro foi cumprida, e Deus comanda Avraham a dar ao seu filho um nome específico e a circuncidá-lo (himmolhimmol / המולהמול). Tanto os cumprimentos quanto a obediência ao comando divino são registrados na narrativa.In Avraham’s story, God’s promise of a coming son was fulfilled, and God commands Avraham to give his son a specific name and to circumcise (himmolhimmol / המולהמול) him. Both fulfillments and the obedience to the divine command are registered in the narrative.

Desta forma, a chegada de Yitskhaq e a sua circuncisão fazem soar a nota da narrativa do dilúvio e marcam esta unidade como uma narrativa-dobradiça que conclui o ciclo temático e também inicia um novo ciclo temático.In this way, Yitskhaq's arrival and his circumcision strike the note of the flood narrative and mark this unit as a hinge narrative that concludes the thematic cycle and also begins a new thematic cycle.

The fact that this is immediately followed by God’s rescue of Hagar and Yishmael shows that he has their well-being in mind, even if it means a painful separation. This itself finds a certain analogy with the Eden narrative, as God expels the very humans that he wanted to bless.
