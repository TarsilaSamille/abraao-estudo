Sessão 9: Um Dilúvio de ViolênciaSession 9: A Flood of Violence

Principais LiçõesKey Takeaways

  • Os reis de Gênesis 14:1-10 são retratados como personificações do mal e da corrupção através de seus nomes e outro simbolismo.The kings of Genesis 14:1-10 are portrayed as personifications of evil and corruption through their names and other symbolism.
  • Os reis eliminaram grupos de gigantes, recordando os Nefilins, cuja violência arruinou a terra e trouxe o dilúvio.The kings eliminated the groups of giants, recalling the Nephillim whose violence ruined the land and brought on the flood.
  • O resgate dramático de Ló por Avram é retratado como miraculoso, validando a posição de Avram como agente escolhido de Deus.Avram’s dramatic rescue of Lot is depicted as miraculous, validating Avram’s status as God’s chosen agent.

Tradução e Design Literário de Gênesis 14:1-16Translation and Literary Design of Genesis 14:1-16

1

E aconteceu nos dias de Anrafel, rei de Sinar, de Arique, rei de Elasar, de Quedorlaomer, rei de Elão, e de Tidal, rei de Goim (= “nações”),And it came about in the days of Amraphel, king of Shinar, of Aryok, king of Elasar, of Kedorlaomer, king of Elam, and of Tidal, king of Goyyim (= “nations”),

2 que fizeram guerra contra Bera (= “em mal”), rei de Sodoma, e contra Birsa (= “em perversidade”), rei de Gomorra, e contra Sinabe (= “pai-odiador”), rei de Admá, e contra Semeber (= “nome de destruição”), rei de Zeboim, e contra o rei de Belá (= “devorar”) [esta é Zoar].that they made war with Bera (= “in evil”), king of Sodom, and with Birsha (= “in wickedness”), king of Gomorrah, and with Shinab (= “father hater”), king of Admah, and with Shemeved (= “name of destruction”), king of Tsiboyim, and with the king of Bela (= “devour”) [this is Zoar].

3 Todos estes se uniram no vale de Sidim [este é o Mar de Sal].All of these joined together in the valley of Siddim [this is the Sea of Salt].

4 Doze anos haviam sido escravos de Quedorlaomer, e no décimo terceiro ano se rebelaram.For twelve years they had been slaves of Kedorlaomer, and in the thirteenth year they rebelled.

5 E no décimo quarto ano Quedorlaomer foi, e os reis que com ele estavam, e feriram os refains em Asterote-Carnaim (= “Asherah de Dois-Picos”), e os zuzins em Ham, e os emins em Savé-Quiriatain (= “a Planície das Duas-Cidades”),and in the fourteenth year Kedorlaomer went, and the kings who were with him, and they struck the Rephaim in Ashteroth-Qarnayim (= “Asherah of Twin-Peaks”), and the Zuzim in Ham, and the Emim in Shaveh-Qiryathayim (= “the Plain of Twin-Cities”),

6 e os horitas nos montes de Seir, até o carvalho de Parã, que está junto ao deserto.and the Horites in the mountains of Seir, unto the Oak of Paran which is by the wilderness.

8 E o rei de Sodoma saiu, e o rei de Gomorra, e o rei de Admá, e o rei de Zeboim, e o rei de Belá [esta é Zoar], e ordenaram batalha contra eles no vale de Sidim;And the king of Sodom went out, and the king of Gomorrah, and the king of Admah, and the king of Tsiboyim, and the king of Bela [this is Zoar], and they lined themselves up for war in the valley of Siddim;

9 contra Quedorlaomer, rei de Elão, e Tidal, rei de Goim, e Anrafel, rei de Sinar, e Arique, rei de Elasar — quatro reis contra cinco.against Kedorlaomer, king of Elam, and Tidal, king of Goyyim, and Amraphel, king of Shinar, and Aryok, king of Elasar — four kings against five.

10 Ora, o vale de Sidim estava cheio de muitas covas de betume; e fugiram o rei de Sodoma e o de Gomorra, e caíram ali; e os que restaram fugiram para os montes.Now, the valley of Siddim was full of many pits of tar. And they fled, the king of Sodom, and Gomorrah, and they fell into there. And those remaining fled to the mountains.

11

E tomaram todos os bens de Sodoma e Gomorra, e toda a sua comida, e se foram.And they took all the possessions of Sodom and Gomorrah, and all their food, and they went away.

12 E tomaram a Ló, e os seus bens, filho do irmão de Avram, e se foram; pois ele habitava em Sodoma.And they took Lot and his possessions, the son of the brother of Avram, and they went away. Now, he was dwelling in Sodom.

13 E veio um fugitivo, e o anunciou a Avram, o hebreu; ora, ele habitava junto aos carvalhos de Manre, o amorreu, irmão de Escole e de Aner, os quais eram membros da aliança com Avram.And a refugee came and reported to Avram the Hebrew; now, he was dwelling among the oaks of Mamre the Amorite, brother of Eshkol, and brother of Aner, who were members of a covenant with Avram.

14 E Avram ouviu que seu irmão tinha sido levado cativo, e trouxe seus homens treinados, nascidos de sua casa, trezentos e dezoito, e perseguiu-os até Dã.And Avram heard that his brother had been taken captive, and he brought out his trained men, those born of his house, three hundred and eighteen, and he chased them unto Dan.

15 E dividiu-se contra eles de noite, ele e seus servos, e os feriu, e os perseguiu até Hobá, que fica ao norte de Damasco.And he divided himself against them by night, he and his servants, and he struck them, and he chased them unto Khobah, which is north of Damascus.

16 E trouxe de volta todos os bens, e também a Ló, seu irmão, e os seus bens, e também as mulheres, e o povo.And he brought back all the possessions, and also Lot his brother, and his possessions, and also the women, and the people.

GEN 14:1-10
The names have been listed in symmetrical pattern, which places the Mesopotamian kings at the outer locations and highlights the giant clans in the center position. Each of these design features seems intentional and communicates something significant. The inverted Mesopotamian kings: The two kings highlighted are the rulers of Shinar and Elam. Both kingdoms play a significant role in the larger biblical story.

This precise linking of the kings to the list of Noah’s sons in Genesis 10 is intentional.
