Yaaqov’s Sons in the Biblical ImaginationYaaqov’s Sons in the Biblical Imagination

The stories found in Genesis 37-50 bring the narrative of the Genesis scroll to its climax, as Yaaqov’s family is nearly destroyed by the jealous rivalry of his sons. The older brothers’ envy of the youngest brother, Yoseph, sets in motion a chain of events that ultimately results in the salvation of the family from a terrible famine. The PointThe Point

Later biblical authors see the story of Yoseph, Yehudah, and the division and reconciliation of the family as a forward preview of the entire history of Israel leading up to the messianic age. These past stories were designed to display a pattern that kept repeating itself through Israel’s history, leading up to the time of Jesus and the apostles. For the biblical authors, the key to understanding their present and future was found in these narrative about their past. The past anticipates the future, and the present maps onto what has come before and what will come later.Later biblical authors see the story of Yoseph, Yehudah, and the division and reconciliation of the family as a forward preview of the entire history of Israel leading up to the messianic age.
