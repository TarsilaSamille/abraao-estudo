Session 14: Reflecting on God’s Character

Key Takeaways
The book of Jonah helps you build the skills you need to read the rest of the Bible.
Jonah flees God's call because he doesn't see how God can be merciful and loving and still just.
A portrait of God being fully just and fully merciful is represented by Jesus on the cross.

This session has no notes

Reflection Question
What questions do you have about the aspects of God’s character found in Exodus 34:6-7? Do you struggle
with the tension between God’s justice and mercy? Why or why not?
