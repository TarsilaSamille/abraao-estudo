Session 1: Relearning the Story of Jonah

Key Takeaways
Reading the Bible well requires unlearning and relearning what the text has to say.
Jonah is a microcosm of all the beauty, potential, and challenges the Bible represents to its readers.
Jonah is more than a children’s story. It’s like a symphony—anyone can sit down and enjoy it, but if you
want to learn how it’s composed, there are infinite layers of complexity to explore.
