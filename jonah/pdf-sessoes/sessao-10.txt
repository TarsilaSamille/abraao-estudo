Session 10: Hyperlinks in the Text

Key Takeaways
The biblical authors often use words that are meant to recall other parts of Scripture. In this way, one
word or image can be loaded with layers of meaning.
The scroll of Jonah starts with the Hebrew word for “and.” This signals to readers that Jonah has a bigger
context and is connected to what came before.
