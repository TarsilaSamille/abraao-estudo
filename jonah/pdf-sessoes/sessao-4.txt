Session 4: The Main Message of the Hebrew
Scriptures

Key Takeaways
The Hebrew Bible is messianic wisdom literature that leads us to understand our need to be rescued by
the Messiah, Jesus.
In 2 Timothy 3:14-17, “good works” refers to seeking the well-being of our neighbors in the name of
Jesus.
We need to build a skill set for reading these texts and examine how we talk about the Hebrew Bible
with other people.

This session has no notes

Reflection Question
Jesus and Paul teach that the Hebrew Bible is all about a messiah who must pass through death, come out the
other side, and create a new humanity (Luke 24:44-47; 2 Tim. 3:14-16). What do you think people in your
context think the Hebrew Bible is about? Are there common objections people might have to the Hebrew
Bible being about the Messiah and his mission?
