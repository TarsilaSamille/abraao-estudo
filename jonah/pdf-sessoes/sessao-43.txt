Session 43: The Meaning of Key Terms in Jonah

Key Takeaways
God wants to deliver Jonah from his distorted mindset where his enemies finding mercy is evil to him.
The imagery of Jonah with two shelters, one made by him and one provided by God, tells us he has a
choice to make. Will Jonah stay in his own version of reality, or will he accept the version of reality that
God wants to give him as a gift?
