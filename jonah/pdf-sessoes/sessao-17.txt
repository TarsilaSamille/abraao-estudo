Key Takeaways
Biblical narrative achieves maximal characterization through minimal details. The authors do a lot with a
little, which is why everything matters.
Symmetrical design is when repeated words link passages that are not side by side, prompting the
reader to compare the passages. All four chapters of Jonah are symmetrically designed.

The Flow of Jonah
Repeated words in Jonah’s literary units develop a thematic flow and lend momentum to the story arc.
