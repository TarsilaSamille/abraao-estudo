Session 5: Reflecting on How To Read the Bible

Key Takeaways
We’re meeting another mind when we read the Bible, so we have to work hard to make sure we’re not
hearing our own voice reflecting back to us.

This session has no notes

Reflection Question
What might it look like for you to embody, reflect, and model the importance of the Hebrew Bible to the
people in your context (ministry, church, neighborhood, family, etc.)? How could you invite other people into
the story that defined reality for Jesus?
