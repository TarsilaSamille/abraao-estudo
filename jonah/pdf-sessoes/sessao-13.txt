Session 13: God’s Character Summarized

Key Takeaways
Exodus 34:6-7 is a dense statement of the character of God, and it is the most quoted verse in the Old
Testament, including a reference in Jonah 4.
In this passage, God explains that he offers mercy to sinful people out of his lovingkindness.
Two important characteristics of God are his justice and his mercy.
