Session 20: Hyperlinks in Star Wars and in Jonah

Key Takeaways
Universal narratives, like the ones in the Bible, help us identify with characters and their stories,
connecting them to our own stories.

Relationships Between Star Wars Characters
In the Star Wars movies, characters are given similar characteristics or narrative moments in order to create
plot tension and expectation. The viewer is intended to pick up on these similarities and begin to connect,
compare, and contrast these characters so that the viewer is drawn deeper into the story. The Bible’s authors
utilize similar storytelling techniques in crafting the biblical narrative.
