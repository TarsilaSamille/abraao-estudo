Session 11: Reflecting on Nineveh

Key Takeaways
Nineveh is called "that great city" twice in the Hebrew Bible. The biblical authors use this unique phrase
to connect the events in Jonah to the founding of Babylon.
The Hebrew word gadol, translated as "great," means large in size or significance.
