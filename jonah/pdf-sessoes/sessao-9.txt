Session 9: Reflecting on Biblical Patterns

Key Takeaways
The Hebrew Bible trains us, through repeated patterns, to expect humans to fail to live up to their divine
calling.
This repeated pattern shows humanity’s need for a divine-human rescuer who will do what only God
can do.
The incarnation of Jesus is not a surprise twist; it’s the only possible solution.
