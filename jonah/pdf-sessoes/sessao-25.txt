Session 25: Reflecting on Jonah’s Motives

Key Takeaways
The biblical authors intentionally leave out information about characters’ motives to invite us to think
about our own experiences.
Jonah is depicted as completely unaware, as if his rebellion has put him in a stupor.

This session has no notes

Reflection Question
The biblical authors don't often give you all the information that you need to make sense of characters’
motives in the story. What thoughts or questions do you have about Jonah’s motives?
