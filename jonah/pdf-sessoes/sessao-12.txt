Key Takeaways
The Hebrew Bible has one storyline that repeats over and over. Knowing this pattern helps us
understand the story of Jonah.
The storyline of the Hebrew Bible is about God choosing a people to bless all the nations and
responding to the outcry of violence and evil by rescuing them through the waters. Then, a chosen one
intercedes for the wicked so that God’s covenant blessing can go out to the nations.
