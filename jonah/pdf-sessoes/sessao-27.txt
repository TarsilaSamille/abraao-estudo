The Contrast Between Jeremiah and Jonah
In this scene, the sailors cry out to Yahweh, asking that he not hold them guilty for killing an innocent man.
The language of this scene hyperlinks Jeremiah 26, but the roles are reversed. Instead of a righteous prophet
and rebellious people, Jonah is a rebellious prophet and the sailors are acting righteously.
