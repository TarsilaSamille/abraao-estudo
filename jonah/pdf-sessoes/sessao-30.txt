Session 30: Reflecting on the Sea Monster

Key Takeaways
The point of Jonah isn’t to relay security camera footage of what happened to him. The point of the
book is the meaning of what happened to him.
God chose to reveal himself through the ancient Israelites and their framework of the world. So if we
want to understand the Bible, we have to understand their framework.
