Session 19: Reflecting on Seeing the Hyperlinks

Key Takeaways
The Hebrew Bible gives us a realistic portrait of the human condition through a litany of stories that
build on the Eden narrative. And ultimately, this portrait draws our attention to Jesus.
To identify hyperlinks between stories, look for clusters of shared words or phrases, shared language
that consists of distinct vocabulary, shared language within thematically similar contexts, and shared
language between units that are united by other literary strategies (similar plot, themes, settings).
