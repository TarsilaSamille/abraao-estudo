Sessão 4: Ansiando pelo Reino de DeusSession 4: Longing for God’s Kingdom

Principais LiçõesKey Takeaways

  • Jesus explica que aqueles que choram têm a boa vida porque serão consolados. Isso espelha as palavras de Isaías 61:2, onde o conforto é prometido àqueles que choram pela infidelidade de Israel à aliança.Jesus explains that those who mourn have the good life because they will be comforted. This mirrors the words of Isaiah 61:2, where comfort is promised to those mourning over Israel’s covenant unfaithfulness.
  • As três primeiras bem-aventuranças destacam a esperança para aqueles que são impotentes, que sofrem com a corrupção, e que são frequentemente ignorados, sem voz ou influência. Isso enfatiza o foco de Deus nos marginalizados.The first three beatitudes highlight hope for those who are powerless, who grieve over corruption, and who are often overlooked, having no voice or influence. This emphasizes God’s focus on the marginalized.
  • A segunda tríade de bem-aventuranças destaca o caráter de pessoas que anseiam ver seu mundo refletir o coração de Deus através de relacionamentos corretos, misericórdia e corações puros.The second triad of beatitudes highlights the character of people longing to see their world reflect God’s heart through right relationships, mercy, and pure hearts.

The difficult circumstances will be painful and unpleasant (think of the early chapters of Acts of the Apostles), and so his disciples will constantly need to reframe such hardship. Persecution, Jesus says, is not a sign of divine disapproval—just the opposite. It is a sign of honor, showing that the message of Jesus’ Kingdom is confronting the powers and provoking a predictable response of hostility.
