Session 16: Reflecting on Matthew 1-4
Key Takeaways
Matthew’s use of Hebrew Bible quotes invites us to meditate on—that is, to read and reread—the unified
story of the Bible through the revealing lens of Jesus coming to this world.
The Bible continually offers ways to see the familiar in new, unfolding layers of depth and meaning.
This text is not just about words, it’s about people. It was created in community and was intended to be
studied in community and to lead us to a person.
