Session 10: The Nazarene

Key Takeaways
Quoting from Hosea, who himself frames Israel’s situation in the language and imagery of the exodus,
Matthew continues the tradition of interpreting current events through the lens of Israel’s Scriptures.
The deeper layers of meaning Matthew constructs through these references enrich the more direct
parts of his message. The text offers much from a simple reading, and it holds riches for a lifetime of
deep meditation.
The term Nazoraios (Nazarene, Matt. 2:23) may connect Jesus to the promise of a “branch/shoot” from
the line of David (Isa. 11:1), or it may link him to the Nazirite vow (Num. 6:1-21), a most holy commitment.
The ambiguity may invite us to make both comparisons.
